Exclusion
 ○ A jump to the five level in a new suit after a
   suit is agreed asks for key cards outside the
   bid suit. Responses follow 1430 starting at the
   next step.

 ○ Italian style: first- or second-round controls,
   bid up the line, so a bypassed suit denies a
   control in it.
 ○ The first control bid below game asks partner
   to cooperate with the cheapest control;
   returning to the trump suit denies one.
